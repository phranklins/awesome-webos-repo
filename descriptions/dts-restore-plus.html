For rooted TVs. Restores or changes audio/firmware-related behavior. Read the project README.

Restore DTS and Dolby TrueHD/MLP audio on rooted LG webOS 25 TVs, including DTS in mp4/ts/m2ts containers. Also offers exact-firmware experimental DTS support for C2/G2 via explicit opt-in. Requires the Homebrew Channel + root.

Project page: https://github.com/josippapez/dts_restore_plus
