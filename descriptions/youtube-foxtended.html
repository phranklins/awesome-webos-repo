Same app id as webosbrew YouTube AdFree and the other YouTube homebrew forks (youtube.leanback.v4). Only one of these three can be installed at a time; installing another replaces or conflicts with the one you already have. Uninstall the official LG YouTube app before installing any of them.

Ad-free YouTube for webOS 3 and newer, with SponsorBlock, Return YouTube Dislike and DeArrow.
